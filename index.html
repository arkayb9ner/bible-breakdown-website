Bible Breakdown · A Christian Armory project

The whole Bible, one readable section at a time.

Every one of the 73 books of the Catholic Bible, broken into sections sized to the book itself. Genesis — fifty chapters — gets fifteen sections. A one-chapter letter like 2 John gets one. No book is too big to start, and no book is skipped.

What’s inside every section

Each section follows the same four-part shape, so you always know what you’re getting — whether the section covers eleven chapters or a single page.

1

What happens

A plain-English summary of the passage — the story told straight, in words anyone can follow.

2

Key verses

The verses that carry the section, quoted word-for-word from the Catholic Public Domain Version (CPDV) — the same Bible text used on the Christian Armory.

3

Themes

The big ideas running through the section — what it teaches about God, about us, and about how we’re called to live.

4

One reflection question

A single question to sit with after reading — for prayer, journaling, or discussion.
